# Res Arcana - Game Rules

## Overview

2-4 alchemical mages vie for control of ancient monuments and Places of Power. Use magical essences and items to build artifacts, activate their powers, and command dragons. The mage with the most points (10+) wins!

## Essences

Five types of magical essences:
- **Elan** (Red)
- **Life** (Green)
- **Calm** (Blue)
- **Death** (Black)
- **Gold** (Yellow)

## Components

### Component Types

A **component** is an artifact, mage, magic item, monument, or Place of Power.

- **Mage**: Begins in play. Not an artifact, cannot be destroyed.
- **Artifact**: Placed from hand by paying cost. Some are also Dragons and/or Creatures.
- **Magic Item**: Chosen during setup, swapped when passing.
- **Monument**: Claimed from center for 4 Gold. Cannot be destroyed.
- **Place of Power**: Claimed from center by paying cost. Cannot be destroyed.

### Component States

- **Straightened**: Ready to use powers
- **Turned**: Sideways, powers cannot be used (except some React powers)

## Setup

1. Put 8 magic items, essence supply, and 5x essence chips in the center
2. Set out 5 Places of Power (for first game use "A" sides; later games randomly choose sides)
3. Shuffle monuments, set out 2 face-up, form draw pile with rest
4. Give each player 1 of each essence (including Gold) = 5 essences total
5. Select First Player, who takes First Player token
6. Shuffle mages and artifacts separately. Deal 2 mages and 8 artifacts to each player. Return others to box.
7. Each player examines their 10 cards, then:
   - Shuffles their 8 artifacts to form their deck
   - Draws 3 artifacts as initial hand
   - Selects 1 mage (reveal simultaneously, return unused to box)
8. In counter-clockwise order starting right of First Player, each player chooses 1 magic item

## Round Structure

A game typically lasts 4-6 rounds. Each round has 3 phases:

### Phase 1: Collect Essences

Each player (can be simultaneous):
1. Execute all Collect abilities on their components
2. May take essences from their components

**Collect Ability Rules:**
- If essence shows a number, take that many; otherwise take 1
- "+" separating essences = take all of them
- "/" separating essences = choose 1 of them
- Gray pentagon with number = choose any mix of essences equal to that number
- Restrictions (crossed-out icons) = cannot choose those essence types

**Taking Essences from Components:**
- You may take essences off some components and not others
- If you take ANY essences off a component, you must take ALL of them
- Taking all essences from a Place of Power loses those victory points

**Special Cases:**
- Cursed Forge: Pay 1 Death OR turn the Place of Power
- Vault: If Gold left on it (not taken), gain 2 non-Gold essences
- Windup Man: If essences left on it (not taken), add 2 of each color on it

### Phase 2: Actions

Starting with First Player, clockwise, each player takes 1 action until all pass.

**Actions:**

1. **Place an Artifact**
   - Pay placement cost (shown in upper left of card)
   - Place artifact in front of you, straightened
   - Discounts are cumulative; cost cannot go below 0

2. **Claim a Monument**
   - Pay 4 Gold
   - Take one of the 2 face-up monuments OR top of monument deck
   - Replace from deck if taking face-up monument

3. **Claim a Place of Power**
   - Pay placement cost shown on card
   - Once claimed, cannot be taken by another player

4. **Discard an Artifact**
   - Discard from hand (tuck sideways under deck)
   - Gain 1 Gold OR any 2 non-Gold essences

5. **Use a Power**
   - Must be on a straightened component
   - Pay cost, apply effect
   - If cost includes turning, turn the component sideways
   - Powers without turning in cost can be used multiple times (each is a separate action)

6. **Pass**
   - If first to pass: take First Player token (flip to Passed side)
   - Swap magic item for a different one in supply (flip to Passed side)
   - Draw 1 card (reshuffle discards if deck empty)
   - Cannot take more actions this round
   - Ignores all Life loss but still gains essences from rivals' powers

### Phase 3: Check Victory

After all players pass:
- If any player has 10+ points: game ends, most points wins
- **Tiebreaker**: Most essences in pool (Gold counts as 2)
- If still tied: shared victory
- If no one has 10+ points: straighten all components, flip tokens from Passed side, begin next round

## Powers

Powers have a **cost** and an **effect**, separated by a triangle symbol.

### Cost Elements
- Turn symbol = turn this component sideways
- Essence icons = pay those essences to supply
- "+" = pay all parts
- Special costs (destroy artifact, discard card, turn a Dragon, etc.)

### Effect Elements
- Essence icons = gain those essences
- "on" with down arrow = place on this component (not in pool)
- Special effects (straighten, draw cards, Life loss, etc.)

### React Powers

- Triggered by a specific condition (e.g., "React to Life loss")
- Used out of turn, not an action
- CAN be used when component is turned, UNLESS cost requires turning
- Common triggers: Life loss, victory check

## Life Loss

When an effect inflicts Life loss on rivals:
1. Rivals who have passed ignore it
2. Each affected rival must lose the indicated number of Life essences
3. For each Life they cannot pay: lose 2 other essences instead (any type including Gold)
4. Many Life loss effects give rivals a React option to ignore (at a cost)

## Destroy vs Discard

- **Discard**: From hand. Place in discard pile (sideways under deck).
- **Destroy**: From play. Place in discard pile. Essences on destroyed card return to supply. Turned artifacts can be destroyed.

## Discounts

Placement discounts (Artificer, Dragon Bridle, Dragon's Lair, etc.):
- Apply only to their specified targets
- Are cumulative with each other
- Cannot reduce cost below 0

## Drawing Cards

- If deck is empty when you need to draw, reshuffle discards to form new deck
- Effects drawing 3 cards (Divination, Hawk, Oracle): if fewer than 3 in deck after reshuffling, draw all available

## Victory Points

Points come from:
- Artifacts (some have point values)
- Monuments (2-3 points each)
- Places of Power (base points + points from essences on them)
- First Player token (1 point)

**Special Victory Rules:**
- Coral Castle, Sorcerer's Bestiary: Can trigger immediate victory check mid-round
- Golden Statue: React power gives 3 temporary points during victory check (costs 3 Gold)
- Cursed Forge, Sacred Grove, Sacrificial Pit: Have base points PLUS points for essences

## Component-Specific Rules

### Mages
- **Artificer**: Discount applies only to artifacts (not PoP or Monuments)
- **Transmuter**: Can take same essence types as turned in. Requires 2+ essences in pool.

### Artifacts
- **Athanor**: Two powers - build Elan on it; convert 6 Elan FROM CARD + X same essences to X Gold
- **Corrupt Altar**: Second power can destroy itself
- **Crypt**: Places artifact from discard pile. If cost is Gold + 1 other, reduce to just Gold.
- **Fiery Whip**: Second power cannot target itself
- **Guard Dog**: First power (straighten self) CAN be used when turned
- **Mermaid**: Places 1 Calm/Life/Gold from YOUR pool on one of YOUR components
- **Sacrificial Dagger**: Second power requires BOTH destroying self AND discarding a card
- **Vault**: Collect ability gives 2 non-Gold IF Gold on Vault and you don't take it
- **Windup Man**: Collect ability adds 2 of each color IF you leave essences on it

### Magic Items
- **Transmutation**: Can take same essence types as turned in. Requires 3+ essences in pool.

### Monuments
- **Golden Statue**: React points are temporary (only during that victory check)

### Places of Power
- **Cursed Forge**: During Collect, pay 1 Death OR turn it
- **Dragon's Lair**: Power requires turning BOTH this AND a Dragon
- **Sacred Grove**: Power requires turning BOTH this AND a Creature
- **Sorcerer's Bestiary**: Sea Serpent counts as 3 points (both Dragon and Creature)

## Glossary

- **Ability**: Collect effect, placement discount, or victory formula that applies automatically
- **Creature**: A type of artifact
- **Dragon**: A type of artifact
- **Essence Pool**: Essences a player has that are not on components
- **Rivals**: Your opponents (not you)
- **Straighten**: Turn a sideways component upright
- **Turn**: Turn a component sideways (usually as part of power cost)
